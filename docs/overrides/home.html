Research Directions

  • Music, Audio & Auditory Neuroscience

    How the brain encodes and responds to music and speech: identifying songs and listeners from EEG, what makes music enjoyable or aesthetically rated, and decoding speech and auditory perception from neural signals.

  • Brain-Computer Interfaces & Neural Decoding

    Building BCIs for motor imagery and stroke rehabilitation, and decoding EEG into images, text, and cognitive states with deep learning, including continual and adaptive learning approaches for real-world BCI use.

  • Meditation, Mindfulness & Contemplative Neuroscience

    Characterizing brain-network changes across meditation traditions, classifying meditator expertise and mind-wandering from EEG, and building real-time neurofeedback systems for practice.
